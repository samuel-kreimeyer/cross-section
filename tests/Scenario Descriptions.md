# Scenario Descriptions

Scenarios are named in the subheadings and descriptions of the components follow. Scenarios can be visualized in the `example sections` folder under file names that match the sections below. Note examples should be created with the cross-section core API and not directly constructed from SVG primatives. Each section should include annotations.

## ARDOT Undivided Highway

Undivided highway with two 11ft lanes paved with two 2in surface courses and a 3in asphalt binder course on top of 6in of aggregate base. On both sides are 4'  shoulders where 2ft is paved with only 2in asphalt pavement on 11in of aggregate base, and the remaining 2ft is an aggregate extension. The surface and subgrade both have 2% cross slopes. The shoulders have 4% slopes on the paved section and 4:1 fill slopes. The Fill slopes extend to 16ft from the edge of traveled way on both sides. The left side has a 3:1 back slope.

## ARDOT Undivided notch and widen

Undivided highway with 20ft of 2in asphalt overlay. Existing pavement is cut with an 11in notch. The two 11ft lanes are completed with 1ft of full depth pavement of two-courses of 2in asphalt surface and one 3in course of asphalt binder on 6in of aggregate base. The shoulders are 4ft wide with a 4% cross slope, and the same pavement section for 2ft and an aggregate extension for two feet. The cut off slope for the shoulders is 4:1. The fill slopes behind the shoulder are 4:1 to an offset of 16ft from the edge of traveled way (ETW is 11ft from centerline)
